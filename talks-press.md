## TALKS & PRESS [in progress]

### Talks & Workshops

[Neither Her Nor Hal](https://www.slideshare.net/EmilySaltz/neither-her-nor-hal-considering-access-and-representation-in-the-next-generation-of-speech-technology) - Considering Access and Representation in the Next Generation of Speech Technology at Processing Community Day in Los Angeles (2019)

[New Frontiers in the Sonification of Financial Data](http://www.csun.edu/cod/conference/2018/sessions/index.php/public/presentations/view/236) - A presentation of Bloomberg/CMU accessibility user research and design at the CSUN Assistive Tech Conference (2017)

[Podcast Search API: Audiosear.ch](http://mediashift.org/2015/08/meet-the-8-audio-podcasting-projects-presenting-at-collabspace-san-francisco/) - Presented Audiosear.ch podcast search API at the Collab/Space San Francisco meetup hosted by KQED and Mediashift (2015)

### Writing

[Speech Recognition for Media: It's Not All About Accuracy (PBS Mediashift)](http://mediashift.org/2014/08/speech-recognition-for-media-its-not-all-about-accuracy/) - A call for publishers to consider the benefits of imperfect machine-generated transcripts

[Potato is a Mass Noun](https://tinyletter.com/potato-is-a-mass-noun) - My newsletter about language, life, and technology

[Popcast by Pop Up Archive](https://itunes.apple.com/us/podcast/popcast/id970535981?mt=2) - Script editing and creative direction for found sound podcast with stories ranging from 1904 wax cylinder songs to NASA's interstellar rumbles

[Using Speech Technology for Political Analysis (Medium)](https://medium.com/@PopUpArchive/what-we-found-by-using-speech-technology-for-political-analysis-5c402d28ad53) - In this project the Pop Up Archive team took a look at how text analysis tools could bring insight about the speech patterns and key topics during an especially crowded Oakland mayoral race

[Prototyping With Data: Visualizing Environmental Triggers Of Asthma (Medium)](https://medium.com/children-and-asthma-service-design-with-philips/prototyping-with-data-visualizing-environmental-triggers-of-asthma-3c43216784bf) - A blog post about exploratory data visualization of asthma triggers for an asthma service design interface

[What are the Top Challenges Facing Audio in 2015? (Medium, PBS Mediashift)](https://medium.com/@PopUpArchive/what-are-the-top-challenges-facing-digital-audio-in-2015-part-one-4483fc2dc498) - A two-part collection of quotes from podcast and radio influencers surveying challenges facing spoken word audio in the digital landscape, created in collaboration with Anne Wootton

### Hackathons

[Morse Striker (Google Creative Lab/Adaptive Design)](https://experiments.withgoogle.com/morse-striker) - A spy-themed soccer game developed with a student with motor and visual diabilities

[Amviewlate (ArchHacks Health Tech @ WashU)](https://saltzshaker.github.io/amviewlate) - Voice-activated augmented reality guide for Parkinson's Disease

[Voxanon (Come and Play Audio Hackathon)](https://hackdash.org/projects/5918b5c24545fa01a8db176d) - VoxAnon is a platform for submitting anonymous audio stories with the ability to use vocal filters to change their voice to share their story anonymously, or to modify their voice for fun

[A-to-V (Open Audio Weekend from The Moth & NYPL)](https://www.nypl.org/blog/2016/07/07/open-audio-weekend-hackathon) - A-to-V is a tool for users to upload their oral history audio collections onto a searchable map
