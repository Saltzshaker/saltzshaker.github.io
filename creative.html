2026
Questions Asked on Self-Help Cassettes (Small Batch Data Market)
Hypotheticals, hallucinations, hypnosis. A dataset of questions clipped from self-help, hypnosis and relaxation tapes found on YouTube. Side A: the music. Side B: the questions. Shown at Small Batch Data Market, LARPA, Bushwick.
2025
The 19% (Salt Institute)
An essay film exploring Maine's civic culture, including declining public meetings and a contentious referendum on voter ID and absentee voting. Shown at the Portland Museum of Art.
2025
Marsh Periscope (Salt Institute)
A mockumentary set in Maine's Scarborough Marsh documenting the breakdown of a DIY periscope.
2022
Games for Bodies: A Collection of Impossible Improv Exercises (NeurIPS AI Art Gallery)
12 improv games generated by GPT-2, fine-tuned on improv games collected by Pamela Fox. Shown in the NeurIPS 2022 AI Art Gallery for the Workshop on Machine Learning for Creativity and Design.
Video  ·  The games (PDF)  ·  AI & Improv talk (WordHack, 2022)
2021
Bread Symphony
With Katya Rozanova, Max Horwich, and Ashley Jane Lewis. A live jar of bread starter with sensors feeds data into Max/MSP to generate sound. Shown at Berkeley's Refamiliarization exhibit, NYC's Electroacoustic Improvisation Summit, the Images Festival in Toronto, and MOCO 2022.
2021
ALGOBOG special (Discobog on WFMU)
Three-hour generative music special for my weekly radio show featuring live AI-generated audio and visuals.
2021
These Eyes Do Exist (Science Gallery Detroit/MSU)
With Lia Coleman. An installation exploring four stages of the AI art creation process (dataset creation, model code, training resources, and publishing), showing how AI is grounded in interdependent, rarely credited human labor. Shown in the fall 2021 Tracked and Traced exhibition.
2019
Super Sad Googles (Code Societies @ SFPC)
Autocomplete populated by crowdsourced "sad" Google searches.
2018
Filter Bubble Roulette (Winner of Mozilla's Reality Redrawn Challenge)
An experience to inhabit user-specific social media feeds based on volumetric video interviews with a range of New Yorkers. Exhibited at the Tech Museum of Innovation.
2016
Close (but not too close)
An interactive installation using depth-sensors to explore the concept of proxemics with a virtual figure.
2016
Into the Mode
A web simulation of an early mode-based text editor which explores an alternate history of computer-based text editing.
