## RESEARCH & DESIGN

### 2026

[How AI Companies Are Handling Suicide and Self-Harm Today (Partnership on AI)](https://partnershiponai.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PAI_how-AI-handles-suicide.pdf) - Co-authored with Claire Leibowicz: an in-depth analysis of how frontier AI companies detect and respond to users in suicide and self-harm crises, including a taxonomy of six common intervention types and open questions for the field. Builds on the [Cross-Sector Primer for AI and Suicide Prevention](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04321) and an [expert workshop hosted by OpenAI](https://partnershiponai.org/when-a-chatbot-becomes-your-therapist/), which I designed and facilitated through [Saltern Studio](https://saltern.studio/).

[AI and the Future of Digital Public Squares (Collective Intelligence)](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/26339137261459441) - Led the community moderation section of a review article on how large language models could strengthen or undermine digital public squares, developed from a convening of civil society experts and technologists.

### 2025

[Facilitation in the AI Era: A Community Roadmap for Technologies to Support Practitioners (Jigsaw)](https://services.google.com/fh/files/blogs/jigsaw_facilitation_in_the_ai_era.pdf) - Lead co-author, with Ian Beacock, of a collaborative report with 13 global experts in facilitating dialogue on how AI could best support public conversations.

[An Ecological Framework for AI and Community Moderation (EPIC)](https://anthrosource.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/pdfdirect/10.1111/epic.70002) - Case study paper on ethnographic research and codesign with community moderators to develop LLM-based moderation tools.

[Sensemaking Tools in Bowling Green, Kentucky (Jigsaw)](https://medium.com/jigsaw/how-one-of-the-fastest-growing-cities-in-kentucky-used-ai-to-plan-for-the-next-25-years-3b70c4fd1412) - Led research with local leaders for a civic deliberation project with 8,000 community members; 83% reported the interactive site helped them understand viewpoints they disagreed with.

### 2024

[Bridging Attributes for Perspective API (Jigsaw)](https://medium.com/jigsaw/announcing-experimental-bridging-attributes-in-perspective-api-578a9d59ac37) - 0-to-1 research with online community members and moderators for classifiers that surface constructive comments, used by organizations like the NYT, Nextdoor, and Viafoura. See also the [evaluation paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.05429).

[Annotator in the Loop: A Case Study of In-Depth Rater Engagement to Create a Prosocial Benchmark Dataset (AIES)](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AIES/article/view/31726) - Co-author of a case study on engaging raters in depth to build a benchmark dataset for prosocial content.

[Independently Testing Prosocial Interventions: Methods and Recommendations from 31 Researchers (Annals of the New York Academy of Sciences)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40579787/) - One of three lead co-authors of cross-disciplinary recommendations for rigorously and independently evaluating prosocial interventions online.

### 2021

[Misinformation Interventions Are Common, Divisive, and Poorly Understood (Harvard Kennedy School Misinformation Review)](https://misinforeview.hks.harvard.edu/article/misinformation-interventions-are-common-divisive-and-poorly-understood/) - Lead author of research on public attitudes toward platform misinformation interventions.

[Labels for Manipulated Media (Partnership on AI and First Draft)](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3411763.3451807) - Foundational user research on labeling interventions for manipulated media, published at CHI.

### 2019–2020

[The News Provenance Project (The New York Times R&D Lab)](https://www.niemanlab.org/2020/01/heres-how-the-new-york-times-tested-blockchain-to-help-you-identify-faked-photos-on-your-timeline/) - UX lead for a blockchain prototype for labeling photojournalism, which went on to inform the [C2PA](https://c2pa.org/) industry standard for verifying media sources.

### 2017–2018

[Stockgrok (Bloomberg/CMU)](https://www.bloomberg.com/ux/2018/08/28/visually-impaired-working-finance-nearly-impossible-bloombergs-ux-designers-working-change/) - Led design research with people with visual impairments for an accessible data sonification tool for financial charts.

[Toward Measuring Empathy in Virtual Reality (CHI PLAY)](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3130859.3131325) - A qualitative user research framework for measuring empathy in VR.

[Can Biosignals Be Expressive?](https://fannieliu.com/papers/can-biosignals-be-expressive.pdf) - Supported research on kinetic typography driven by biosignals.
